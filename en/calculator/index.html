How the calculator works

The base is price per standard page × number of pages. The deadline applies a multiplier: standard 1.0, express (7–13 days) 1.25, sprint (3–6 days) 1.5. From 60 pages there is 5% off, from 100 pages 8%. Add-ons are added at the end without discount.
