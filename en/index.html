Frequently asked

What people ask most often

What is the expert source material for?

For studying the approach, the sources and the argumentation when you write your own work. Submitting someone else's text as your own examination work breaches the rules of academic integrity. Follow the rules of your faculty.

How fast do I get a price?

Immediately, in the calculator on this page. You see the price per standard page, the total for the whole thesis and the effect of the deadline. I send a binding quote within two hours on a working day.

Do you write in Hungarian and English too?

Yes. Slovak is the foundation, but I regularly write in Hungarian and English for Hungarian- and English-taught programmes at Slovak schools, and for study abroad. The price is the same and does not change with the language.

What if the thesis fails the originality check?

I run a similarity check before delivery. If the report shows problematic passages, I revise the text. The similarity percentage is not proof of plagiarism in itself. What matters is whether the sources are cited correctly.

Will you keep my identity confidential?

For a first enquiry, a nickname and an email address for the reply are enough. The message passes through the delivery service named in the privacy notice. Do not send sensitive data or non-anonymised research records.

How do payments work?

By agreement: a single payment or two instalments (for longer theses). You receive an order confirmation with the price and the deadline. For smaller texts there is no advance payment; for large theses, half is paid at the start.

What if I miss the deadline?

Express deadlines are possible: 7–13 days with a 25% surcharge, 3–6 days with 50%. The sooner you write, the less you pay and the better the text will be.

Tell me what you are facing

A few sentences about the topic, your school and the deadline are enough. Within two hours you will know how much it costs and whether we can make it.
