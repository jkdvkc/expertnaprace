Frequently asked

What students ask most

Is it legal to have a thesis written for you?

Writing a text on commission is a legal service — a contract for work. University rules are a different matter: institutions forbid submitting someone else's text as your own work. That is why I deliver research support and model texts that teach structure, argument and source handling. Responsibility for what you submit is always yours.

How fast do I get a price?

Immediately, in the calculator on this page. You see the per-page rate, the total and the effect of the deadline. A binding quote follows within two hours on weekdays.

Do you write in Slovak and Hungarian too?

Yes. Slovak is the main language, but I regularly write for Hungarian- and English-taught programmes at Slovak universities, and for study abroad. The price does not change with language.

What if the thesis fails the similarity check?

I run a similarity check before delivery. If the report shows problematic passages, I rewrite them. A percentage is not proof of plagiarism — citation practice is what matters.

Is it confidential?

Yes. I do not need your full name or address, only an email and your university. Texts are never shown to third parties.

How do payments work?

One payment or two instalments for longer theses, as agreed. You receive a written confirmation with the price and the deadline.

Tell me what you are facing

A few sentences about the topic, your university and the deadline is enough. Within two hours you know the price.
