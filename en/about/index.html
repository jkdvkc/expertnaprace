About the author

Natália

Nine years of academic writing and hundreds of texts, from seminar papers to dissertations. No agency, no call centre — one author who holds the whole text.

It started at university, correcting seminar papers for classmates. It grew into full-time work: literature research, structure, statistics, proofreading. Today I write for students at Slovak universities in Slovak, Hungarian and English.

How I work

  • Every thesis starts with the question of what it is meant to prove.
  • I send the outline for approval before writing the first sentence.
  • I write in parts and send drafts along the way, not one finished text at the end.
  • I never copy text from another thesis. I use sources; the wording is mine.
  • I say so early when a deadline is unrealistic or a topic lacks sources.

Fields

Humanities and social sciences: economics, management, psychology, sociology, education, marketing and public administration. For technical topics I work with a specialist so the text is not only linguistically correct but factually sound.

What you will not get

  • A promised grade. The committee decides, not the author of the text.
  • A claim that the thesis will pass a similarity check before it has been checked.
  • A finished thesis you submit without understanding it — that always comes back at the defence.
The best thesis is one whose author understands every sentence. That is why in consultation I also explain why a sentence is written the way it is.
Three languages, one price: Slovak, Hungarian and English. The price depends on length and deadline, not on language.

Write to me

A few sentences are enough. If it is not my field, I will say so straight away.
