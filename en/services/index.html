Help with the part that cannot wait

You do not have to order a whole thesis. Most people need one specific thing: a structure that works, a survey analysed, a finished text read properly, or preparation for the defence. Pick one.

What people most often come to me for

In nine years of practice I have met many different situations. Some people have a topic but do not know where to start. Others have plenty of academic sources but cannot connect them properly. Often it is also help with methodology, processing research, editing a finished text or preparing for the defence.

I know very well, though, that the problem is not always that you cannot manage the work. Sometimes there is simply no time. I am approached by mothers on maternity leave, parents of small children and people who study alongside a job and family commitments. Combining university, work and family is not easy, and sometimes there is not enough time or energy left for study duties. I approach every client individually. First I look at your brief, at what you have already prepared and at what exactly you need help with. Then we agree on the scope of the collaboration, the deadline and the price.

Seminar papers

A seminar paper is part of university study and is usually written during the term for a particular course. Its purpose is to show that the student can work with academic literature and develop a chosen topic.

Standard length: roughly 5 – 15 pages, depending on the lecturer's requirements.

Professional preparation of source material for seminar papers

I offer professional preparation of expert source material for seminar papers in a wide range of subjects. I can help with a specific part or prepare a complete model text based on your brief, which you can use as a basis for writing your own paper. For every brief I work from the lecturer's requirements, use relevant academic literature and pay attention to correct citation and the language quality of the text.

Bachelor theses

A bachelor thesis is usually written in the final year of the first cycle of university study. With it, the student shows the ability to work independently with academic sources and to apply what they have learned to a specific topic.

Standard length: roughly 30 – 40 standard pages.

Professional preparation of source material for bachelor theses

I offer professional preparation of expert source material for bachelor theses, in full or in part, according to your requirements.

You can turn to me if you need help with the theoretical or practical part, the research methodology or the editing of a text you have already written. If you wish, I will also prepare a complete model text of the thesis as expert source material for your own writing.

I handle every brief individually. I take into account the topic, the required structure, the university's guidelines and the supervisor's comments.

Master theses

A master thesis is written at the end of the second cycle of university study. Compared with a bachelor thesis, greater emphasis is placed on expert depth, methodological soundness, independent treatment of the problem under study and the interpretation of results.

Standard length: roughly 50 – 70 standard pages.

Professional preparation of source material for master theses

I provide professional preparation of expert source material for master theses according to the requirements of your particular university.

I offer full preparation of a model text, theoretical and methodological material, and help with individual parts of the thesis. If you already have an outline, a theoretical part or collected research data, we can focus only on the remaining tasks.

I take care over the expert level, the logical flow between chapters, the relevance of the sources used and compliance with the supervisor's requirements.

Rigorosum theses

A rigorosum thesis is part of the rigorosum procedure, a Central European postgraduate examination (sometimes called a "small doctorate") that usually follows successful completion of a master's degree. The candidate is expected to treat the topic in greater expert depth, work with scholarly sources and use suitable research methods.

Indicative length: roughly 60 – 80 standard pages, depending on the requirements of the faculty.

Professional preparation of source material for rigorosum theses

I offer expert preparation of source material for rigorosum theses, in full or in part.

I can help you with preparing expert chapters, literature reviews, methodology, data analysis and creating a complete model text.

For every brief I take into account the requirements of the rigorosum procedure, the nature of the chosen topic and the professional standards of the field concerned.

Doctoral dissertations

A doctoral dissertation is part of doctoral study (PhD). It is based on independent scientific research whose aim is to bring new knowledge and contribute to the development of the field.

Standard length: roughly 80 – 120 standard pages.

I provide expert support in preparing doctoral dissertations, from systematic literature reviews to methodological consultations, processing of research data and language editing of scientific texts.

I can help with a specific part or with the complete preparation of expert source material. I always adapt the scope of our collaboration to the nature of the research, the requirements of the doctoral programme and the stage the thesis has reached.

Habilitation theses

A habilitation thesis is submitted in the habilitation procedure when applying for the title of docent (associate professor). It may take the form of a scientific monograph, a single-topic work or a set of published scientific papers accompanied by an expert commentary.

Length: individual, according to the university's requirements and the chosen form of the habilitation thesis.

Expert support in preparing habilitation theses

I offer individual expert support in preparing habilitation theses and related documents.

I can help with extensive literature reviews, editorial and language editing of scientific texts, harmonising citations and the formal preparation of material according to the university's requirements.
