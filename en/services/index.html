Help with the part that cannot wait

You do not have to order a whole thesis. Most people need one specific thing: a structure that works, a survey analysed, a finished text read properly, or preparation for the defence. Pick one.

Where people get stuck

In nine years the request almost always narrows to one of five points: I do not know how to structure it; I have a pile of sources and cannot connect them; I have data and do not know what to do with it; the text is finished but does not read well; the defence is coming.

Support, consultation or a finished text?

These are three different things, with different prices and different responsibility:

  • Consultation — we work out the outline, method and sources together. You write the text. The cheapest and safest route.
  • Model text — I write a chapter or a full thesis as a professional example you can learn structure and source work from.
  • Proofreading — a finished text goes through language, style, citations and the format your faculty requires.
Openly: universities forbid submitting someone else's text as your own work. That is why I describe my output as research support and model texts, and never promise a grade. What you submit, and under whose name, is the student's decision.

How the price is calculated

By length, never by PDF pages. One standard page is 1,800 characters including spaces, roughly 250 words. The per-page rate is fixed for each type of work; only the deadline and add-ons change it.

Frequently asked

Can I order just one chapter?

Yes. Most often it is the theory chapter, the methodology or the discussion. The per-page price stays the same; the scope is smaller.

Do you prepare defence presentations?

Yes, from €80, including the talk structure and preparation for the opponent's questions.

How long does 40 pages take?

Two to three weeks on a standard deadline, depending on how difficult the sources are. The schedule is in the quote.
