How it works

  1. You send the brief — five sentences is enough.
  2. Within two hours you get the price, scope and schedule.
  3. If it works, you send the materials and we fix the deadline.
  4. You receive drafts in parts, with room for comments.
  5. At the end: the final text, a similarity check and defence materials.
Confidentiality: no full name or address needed. All communication is by email.
